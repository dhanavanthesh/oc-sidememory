# Provenance

This crate began from Apache-2.0 outlines-core commit
`116a7c381b446814fdfc9b2b28bea7ca4f900aae`.

Inherited files changed here are `src/{error,index,lib,prelude}.rs`,
`src/json_schema/{legacy,mod,types}.rs`, and `src/vocabulary/{mod,processor}.rs`.
`src/primitives.rs` and `src/vocabulary/locator.rs` were relocated without substantive changes.
Each carries a notice.

The compiler, IR, semantic-memory modules, constraints, exact values, transactions, rollback, and
new tests and benchmarks are OC-Sidememory work. See the repository `PROVENANCE.md` for the complete
inventory. OC-Sidememory is independent and is not endorsed by outlines-core.
